#+title: Migrating from tochemfig to chem-mode
#+options: toc:nil num:nil

=chem-mode= is a clean break.  The package does not define the old
=tochemfig= commands or variables.

* Package and loading

| Old                                              | New                   |
|--------------------------------------------------+-----------------------|
| =(require 'tochemfig)=                           | =(require 'chem-mode)= |
| =M-x tochemfig=                                  | =M-x chem-export=     |
| =M-x tochemfig-copy=                             | =M-x chem-copy=       |
| =M-x tochemfig-dispatch=, =tochemfig-custom=     | =M-x chem-dispatch=   |
| =tochemfig-mode=                                 | =chem-mode=           |
| =tochemfig-global-mode=                          | =chem-global-mode=    |

* Optional compatibility snippets

These snippets are not part of =chem-mode=.  Add them to your init after
requiring =chem-mode= if you want the old names locally.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
;;; Commands
(defun tochemfig ()
  "Generate chemfig for the molecule at point or in the region."
  (interactive)
  (let ((chem-default-exporter 'chemfig))
    (chem-export)))

(defalias 'tochemfig-copy #'chem-copy)
(defalias 'tochemfig-dispatch #'chem-dispatch)
(define-obsolete-function-alias 'tochemfig-mode #'chem-mode "0.4")
(define-obsolete-function-alias 'tochemfig-global-mode #'chem-global-mode "0.4")

;;; Variables
(dolist (pair '((tochemfig-default-input . chem-default-input)
                (tochemfig-default-strict . chem-default-strict)
                (tochemfig-default-recalculate-coordinates . chem-default-recalculate-coordinates)
                (tochemfig-default-angle . chem-default-angle)
                (tochemfig-default-flip . chem-default-flip)
                (tochemfig-default-flop . chem-default-flop)
                (tochemfig-default-show-carbons . chem-default-show-carbons)
                (tochemfig-default-show-methyls . chem-default-show-methyls)
                (tochemfig-default-hydrogens . chem-default-hydrogens)
                (tochemfig-default-aromatic-circles . chem-default-aromatic-circles)
                (tochemfig-default-fancy-bonds . chem-default-fancy-bonds)
                (tochemfig-default-atom-numbers . chem-default-atom-numbers)
                (tochemfig-default-bond-scale . chem-default-bond-scale)
                (tochemfig-default-bond-stretch . chem-default-bond-stretch)
                (tochemfig-default-markers . chem-default-markers)
                (tochemfig-default-cross-bond . chem-default-cross-bond)
                (tochemfig-default-entry-atom . chem-default-entry-atom)
                (tochemfig-default-exit-atom . chem-default-exit-atom)
                (tochemfig-default-terse . chem-exp-chemfig-default-terse)
                (tochemfig-default-indent . chem-exp-chemfig-default-indent)
                (tochemfig-default-relative-angles . chem-exp-chemfig-default-relative-angles)
                (tochemfig-default-legacy-lewis . chem-exp-chemfig-default-legacy-lewis)
                (tochemfig-default-wrap-chemfig . chem-exp-chemfig-default-wrap-chemfig)
                (tochemfig-default-submol-name . chem-exp-chemfig-default-submol-name)))
  (defvaralias (car pair) (cdr pair)))
#+end_src

Note: aliasing =tochemfig-mode= only aliases the command.  Code that reads
the =tochemfig-mode= variable still needs to switch to =chem-mode=.

* Minimal options example

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(setq chem-default-angle -30.0)
(setq chem-default-flip t)
(setq chem-exp-chemfig-default-wrap-chemfig t)
#+end_src
